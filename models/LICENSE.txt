The 3D models in this folder (skin.glb, muscles.glb, joints.glb, bones.glb) are adapted from
Z-Anatomy (https://www.z-anatomy.com, https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy) by Gauthier Kervyn
and Lluís Vinent Juanico, itself based on BodyParts3D (© The Database Center for Life Science).

Changes: selected structures, simplified (decimated), tagged with body-region ids in the first
UV channel, split into four layers and compressed for the web (see tools/model-pipeline).
